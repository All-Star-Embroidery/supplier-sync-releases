# All Star Bulk Order Block v1.1.1

Mobile progression and pricing-table UX release.

## Changes

- Hides the redundant large featured image inside expanded products on screens 767px and below. The collapsed product row already provides the product thumbnail and identity, so customers can reach pricing and quantities faster.
- Hides the repeated expanded-product title on mobile while retaining the title in the accordion row.
- Keeps only one product accordion expanded at a time on mobile to reduce excessive page length and make moving between products more predictable. Desktop/tablet multi-open behavior is unchanged.
- Reflows the full per-piece pricing matrix to fit inside the mobile viewport, removing the need to swipe horizontally just to compare quantity tiers.
- Tightens mobile spacing around pricing, decoration, and variant sections.
- Reduces mobile pricing-table typography and padding while retaining all tier values and decoration rows.
- Makes the active-tier notice smaller and easier to scan on phones.
- Compacts decoration-method controls for faster progression.
- Preserves the sticky bottom summary/action bar and all desktop styling.
- Preserves v1.1.0 Supplier Sync compatibility for SanMar, S&S Activewear, Momentec, and multi-supplier WooCommerce variations.
