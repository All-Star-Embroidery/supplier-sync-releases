# All Star Supplier Sync v2.0.14

S&S storefront featured-image ranking improvements.

- Preserves merchant-selected featured images.
- Prefers clean front product photography in Black, Navy, Charcoal, Graphite, Gray/Grey, then White.
- Falls back to any clean front shot, then the S&S style thumbnail, other non-model product angles, and finally the generic product image.
- On-model/lifestyle photography remains gallery-only and is never automatically featured.
- Existing Supplier Sync-managed S&S images are re-evaluated on import/refresh/Quick Repair.
